How the robot moves 
Side view, robot faces right. Left leg = index finger (blue),
right leg = middle finger (green). Knuckle bend = hip swing,
middle-joint bend = knee, tip-joint bend = ankle.
The lowest foot always rests on the bench; the body rises and
sinks to keep it there. Lift one leg and it looks like a step.
Arms swing against the opposite leg. The chest lamp shows state:
green = live, amber = holding or easing back, grey = neutral.
Foot position (fingertip offset) is in the pose data but is not
drawn yet; the robot is driven by the three joint angles.
Calibrate first: hold a relaxed hand still, then press the button.
Joint limits: hip -40..40, knee 0..100, ankle -30..30.
If a direction feels backwards, tell me which joint.

How to read the finger numbers 
Joint angle: 180° = straight finger, smaller = more bent.
3D uses MediaPipe world landmarks (real scale, includes depth).
2D is the flat image angle; it under-reads bending toward the camera.
MCP = knuckle, PIP = middle joint, DIP = near the tip.
Heading: direction of the knuckle-to-tip line on screen
  (0° up, +90° right, 180° down).
Tip: fingertip position relative to the wrist in palm lengths
  (x right, y up, as you see it).
Mirror view changes the picture and the sign of heading and Tip x only.
